🤝 Finding support

You deserve support from real people, not just a website. You could start with a parent or caregiver, another trusted adult, a school counselor, a doctor, or a mental-health professional.

Not sure what to say?

“I've been having a hard time for a while, and I don't feel like myself. I could use some help. Can we talk?”

If the first person doesn't listen, try another trusted adult. You deserve to be heard.

🧠 Understanding depression

Depression is more than ordinary sadness. It can affect mood, interest in activities, energy, sleep, concentration and everyday life. It can happen to teenagers, and it is treatable.

A website can't diagnose or treat depression. If low mood or loss of interest lasts, affects daily life, or worries you, tell a trusted adult and ask for help from a healthcare professional.

🆘 Getting urgent help

In the United States: call or text 988If you are in emotional distress or worried about someone, the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline offers free, confidential support, 24/7. You do not have to be suicidal to contact them. Visit 988 Lifeline.
If there is immediate dangerCall 911 or go to the nearest emergency department with a trusted adult. If you can, tell a parent, caregiver, school counselor or another trusted adult now.
